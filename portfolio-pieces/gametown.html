Game Town

SoMe management, events & Discord

Part-time ∣ Feb 2026 - ongoing

Tools: Canva, Google Workspace, Lovable, a trusty Sony ZV-E10 camera

Platforms: Instagram, LinkedIn, Discord, web

Game Town is both Stockholm’s first coworking space for game developers and a living room for industry professionals, where community events and AWs are hosted regularly. My responsibilities as part-time social media manager are:

  • Posting content about events on Instagram and LinkedIn
  • Managing two Discord servers (community and internal)
  • Updating information on the website + event calendars
  • Assisting with event organization and photography


Reviving Instagram & LinkedIn: Event photos

First things first! To improve our social media presence, I started posting more on our digital platforms. This included setting up a Linktree, highlighting the success of our residents on LinkedIn through reposts, and posting + adding story highlights to Instagram. I also use Canva to design picture collages from our game industry events. Some of them are photographed by me!
This has increased brand visibility and engagement across both Instagram and LinkedIn.

Community Discord management

As the organization grew, I created a dedicated internal Discord server exclusive for residents, complete with customized roles and notification settings. When a new Code of Conduct was introduced, I helped to edit it according to community safety principles and implement it into Discord channels.
On the public community server, I assist in publishing upcoming events and answering common questions. During a period of regular spam incidents, I managed to stop this through configuring Discord’s AutoMod and adding bot-filtering Onboarding questions.

Asset reorganization

Noticing a struggle to quickly locate important files, I took initiative to reorganize the company’s folder structure in Google Workspace (Drive) and Canva to streamline our workflow. By structuring the team’s shared space and folders, we can now find specific photos and saved documents more easily! Take notes, hiring managers: I’ll never miss an opportunity to use alphabetical ordering and color coordination.

Brand concept & marketing strategy

Process: Conducted an segmentation and analysis of the company’s target audience and competitors. After defining Created a marketing concept, visual profile, One Pager, and examples of social media material. Formulated a marketing strategy analyzing the target audience and setting goals for SoMe marketing.
Results: Got a part-time job at Game Town and got to work with my strategy hands-on! As of the middle of 2026, I’ve reached all goals set in the original marketing strategy, but one. (last goal is ongoing)
