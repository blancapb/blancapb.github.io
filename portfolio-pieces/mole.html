MOLE

TikTok content creation & organic growth

Freelance ∣ July - Aug 2026

Tools: TikTok, CapCut, OBS, DaVinci Resolve

Platforms: TikTok, Instagram


Objective

In less than 2 months post-launch, create and post short form content on TikTok for MOLE by Off Black Creations. My task was to create organic growth for the indie horror game, as well as the studios’ unused TikTok account. MOLE has a 98% positive Steam rating, featured in letsplays by large creators like Markiplier, Jacksepticeye and MoistCritical. With no paid ad budget post-launch, my goals were to:

  • Find MOLE’s core audience on TikTok
  • Stand out in the indie horror space among similar titles (competing visually with other low-poly/analog horror games)
  • Reach 1K followers, to be able to add that useful Steam link in bio

In < 2 months, the account reached 650K+ views and grew from 5 followers to more than 5000! Here’s what I did:


Process

Defining target audience

While shaping short form content, I considered the demographics of the game audience: PC gamers between the ages of 18-34 who enjoy indie horror, dark aesthetics and narrative-driven experiences. With a 60/40 female/male split, MOLE has a wide audience overlapping with similar games (Mouthwashing, Iron Lung, Mike Klubnika, Signalis). Along with adapting content, I worked on building a feed for the TikTok account filled with other indie game developers, artists and fans of the horror genre.

Content creation

To start, I used gameplay capture (OBS) and clipping (DaVinci Resolve) to collect video material from the game. From there the videos were adapted into a vertical format and edited using CapCut and TikTok Studio. To drive interaction I used scroll-stopping hooks, simple text overlays, and trending audio tracks, drawing inspiration from promotional strategies used by artists and gamedevs. In captions I targeted aesthetic-driven hashtags like #dreamcore, #liminal and #indiegames, while actively engaging with the community through comment replies, creator reaction reposts, and fan content sharing.

Adapting to analytics

I continuously monitored performance analytics and adapted the content to what worked best. Originally, the work included cross-posting on Instagram Reels, but low engagement prompted me to refocus on TikTok exclusively. This allowed me more time to be active in comment sections! Throughout the two month period, I also gathered analytics data from TikTok Studio to be compiled in a results report later given to Off Black Creations.


Results

Over the 2 month post-launch campaign, Off Black Creations amassed a total of 650K+ views, gained 5K+ followers, 175K likes, and 32K shares. Below you can see some of the best performing TikToks from the account.

#1 performing TikTok

530K views, +3.7K followers gained
“it’s crazy how indie games are mogging big studios rn just through people copying a TikTok link”

Comment threads on this video are filled with community discussion about AAA VS. indie games. Besides encouraging comments, I also used a CTA (“copy link”) to get the video shared and pushed through the algorithm. This resulted in a long lifespan: new views continuing weeks after posting.

#2 performing TikTok

120K views, +1.3K followers gained
“not to be dramatic, but if you watch this 2 times YOU could be the reason our indie game finds its audience”

This video with short gameplay clips used underdog framing (“we’re a small indie team”); inspired by small artists and musicians’ marketing strategies. I used text to make viewers read through the video, creating high engagement. The TikTok also got extra attention through keywords in the caption, referencing games in the same genre.

#3 performing TikTok

15K views
“IT’S HAPPENING 😭 - THE INDIE GAME TAKEOVER”

The day after MOLE was featured in a Markiplier video, I made this TikTok celebrating playthroughs by popular creators and streamers. I included some non-english speaking creators to show the global reach of the game, and used a trending audio track to increase engagement.

Results report

After the project, I provided Off Black Creations with a report containing key analytic findings, future recommendations, and video assets (130 gameplay clips for download that were used in the marketing push).

What worked best?

  • Using a CTA (“copy the link”, “watch this video 2 times”)
  • Low effort content: simple text and short clips (< 2 secs)
  • Music: dark fantasy/backrooms/trending grungy and atmospheric songs
  • Specific hashtags referring to “cores”/aesthetics and interests in indie horror games
  • Not mentioning the game name! A strategy in order to encourage comments early on.
